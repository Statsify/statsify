textures-1.8.json takes 1.8's block and item ids, names and variants from minecraft-data (https://github.com/PrismarineJS/minecraft-data) at 15411f050f040e4c5df09352a75a9912ad8c3eeb, which its README licenses as MIT.

The skill XP tables in leveling.json and the skill stats in stats.json come from Hypixel's SkyBlock skills resource (https://api.hypixel.net/v2/resources/skyblock/skills), version 0.27.2.25. collections.json comes from Hypixel's SkyBlock collections resource (https://api.hypixel.net/v2/resources/skyblock/collections), version 0.27.2.25.

The other JSON files here, and the base, slayer and pet score tables in stats.json, are derived from NEU-REPO (https://github.com/NotEnoughUpdates/NotEnoughUpdates-REPO) at 5a5e9866a8578c8a36891acc508fc1e46e41eb31, under its MIT license:
